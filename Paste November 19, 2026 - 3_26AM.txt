B. Core Billing Engine & FEFO Batch Allocation
Integer Precision Mathematics: All currency calculations (MRP, purchase price, taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST, discounts, round-off, net payable) use integer paise, and quantities use integer thousandths / discrete units to prevent floating-point rounding discrepancies.
Medicine Catalogue vs. Batch Stock: Medicines exist at the catalogue level (with medicine-level minimum stock threshold, HSN, and explicit GST configuration), while physical inventory is tracked by individual batch (Batch Number, Expiry Date, Quantity, MRP, Purchase Price).
Automatic FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out):
When staff add a medicine quantity to the cart, the backend automatically allocates from the earliest-expiring non-expired batch.
Multi-Batch Splitting: If the requested quantity exceeds the earliest batch (e.g., selling 15 strips when Batch A has 10 and Batch B has 20), the engine automatically splits the allocation across Batch A (10 strips at Batch A's MRP) and Batch B (5 strips at Batch B's MRP).
Strict Inventory & Tax Guards:
Expired Stock Block: Expired batches are strictly excluded from normal billing and from usable stock totals.
Insufficient Stock Block: Never allow selling more than the currently available non-expired stock.
No Double Deduction: Stock deduction, invoice creation, payment recording, stock movement ledger entry, and audit trail logging execute inside one atomic SQLite transaction.
Explicit GST Configuration Rule: ORVA must never invent or guess GST rates. A medicine with gst_rate_configured = 0 (unconfigured) blocks billing until its GST rate is explicitly configured and verified (distinguishing an unconfigured medicine from an explicitly configured 0% GST exempt medicine).
Invoice Numbering: Sequential financial-year series (e.g., INV/26-27/000001), strictly enforcing the 16-character maximum limit under GST Rule 46 across both prefix length and sequence growth.
Automatic Commit-Time Timestamps: Every invoice, stock entry, return, and ledger record is automatically stamped with the exact date and time at the moment the transaction commits (never typed by staff, and stamped at commit rather than when the screen was opened).
C. Customer Information & GST Compliance Safeguards
Default Retail Speed: Customer Name, Phone Number, Address, and GSTIN are all optional during ordinary retail billing.
Conditional Legal Prompts:
If a Customer GSTIN is entered (B2B transaction), full 15-character GSTIN structural & checksum validation applies, and recipient details are required.
Uses the shop's 2-digit State Code vs. customer GSTIN/place-of-supply State Code to determine Intra-State (CGST + SGST) vs. Inter-State (IGST) tax breakdown.
If an unregistered B2C transaction meets or exceeds statutory thresholds (e.g., ₹50,000+), the system prompts for mandatory customer Name and Address per GST rules.
